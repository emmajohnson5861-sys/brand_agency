# Agency service image prompts

Created with the built-in ImageGen tool for the five services specified by the user. Photographs are illustrative examples, not client commission claims. Original PNGs are preserved locally in the ignored `.publish/service-image-originals` folder.

Five WebP hover previews are delivered at 400 × 272 pixels for the 200 × 136 frames. The brand strategy photograph also has 480 × 480 and 960 × 960 variants for the approach section. Every image uses the site's warm orange, charcoal and ivory palette.

## Logo Design

Asset: `public/images/services/service-logo-design.webp`

Prompt:

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: photographic service preview for a minimal branding agency website. Landscape 3:2 composition, a SINGLE coherent photograph, not a collage or presentation board.
Primary request: A close editorial overhead photograph of a logo design process: one open ivory sketchbook with precise pencil construction studies for the same elegant abstract angular mark, a single finished black-and-orange logo specimen card laid beside it, a fine graphite pencil and small metal ruler. The mark is consistent from sketch to final. The viewer should instantly recognize logo creation.
Art direction: warm realistic editorial studio photography, sophisticated restrained composition, tactile paper and real printed materials, soft side daylight, gentle natural shadows, credible professional design work. Palette is warm orange approximately #ff7400, charcoal black and warm ivory, used with restraint. Keep the main subject large, cleanly separated and centered so it remains readable in a small 200 by 136 pixel hover preview. Quiet neutral background, all relevant objects comfortably inside the frame.
